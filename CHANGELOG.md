# Changelog

What's new in each version, newest first. The app shows this under Settings → What's New, and `scripts/release.sh` uses a version's section as its release notes, so write for students: a few short, plain bullets about changes they'll notice. `release.sh` adds the date.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-10-09

- New app icon: a blue bell with a K, so ScheduleCountdown is easy to spot in Applications.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-09

- The app now updates itself. When a new version is ready, you'll get a message and an "Update Available" line in the menu.
- Settings shows your app version, whether there's an update, and this list of what's new.
- The menu warns you if the schedule might be out of date, like when your Mac has been offline.
- "Check for Schedule Updates" now tells you whether anything changed.
- Easier install: drag the app into Applications, or let it move itself there.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-08

- First version: a countdown to the next bell in the menu bar, today's periods for your lunch, and alternate schedules that update on their own.
