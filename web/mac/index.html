Installing

You only do this once.

  1. Open the download and drag ScheduleCountdown onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open it from Applications. The first time, your Mac blocks it. That's normal for apps that aren't from the App Store. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password if it asks.
